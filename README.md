A 3D flight game in a desktop browser. You fly Kutilang One, a light prop plane,
out of a green valley airfield: take off from Runway 36, thread the amber rings
over the river, the farmland and the pine ridge, then land back home before the
fuel runs out. Three sorties raise the stakes — more rings, lower rings, stronger
crosswind, and dusk on the last one. A hard landing or an empty tank ends the flight.

The valley is procedural and seeded: a river, a town with a church tower to steer
by, pine forest, rolling hills and a ring of peaks. Chase and cockpit cameras, a
lit HUD with altitude, speed, heading, vertical speed, throttle and fuel, an
artificial horizon, synthesised engine and wind sound, and no network requests —
everything runs from plain static files under `game/`.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

**W** / **↑** nose up · **S** / **↓** nose down · **A** **D** / **←** **→** bank & turn ·
**Shift** throttle up · **Ctrl** throttle down · **F** flaps · **C** cockpit camera ·
**P** pause · **R** restart · **M** mute. The mouse steers too: hold the left button
and drag to bank and pitch.
